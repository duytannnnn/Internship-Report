## Sharing and Feedback

## General Evaluation

### 1. Working Environment

During my internship, I experienced a professional, supportive, and collaborative working environment. Team members were always willing to share their knowledge and provide assistance whenever I encountered difficulties. This made me feel comfortable asking questions, discussing ideas, and continuously improving my skills.

### 2. Support from Mentor and Team Admin

My mentor provided clear guidance throughout the internship and was always willing to explain concepts whenever I faced challenges. The feedback and practical experience shared by the mentor helped me better understand AWS Cloud technologies, serverless architecture, and real-world software development practices. The team administrators were also very helpful in providing resources, announcements, and administrative support.

### 3. Relevance to My Academic Major

The tasks assigned during the internship were closely related to my major in Information Technology. In addition to applying knowledge learned at university, I had the opportunity to explore new areas such as Cloud Computing, Artificial Intelligence, and Serverless Application Development, which greatly expanded my technical understanding.

### 4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities

This internship provided valuable opportunities to develop both technical and soft skills. I gained hands-on experience with AWS services such as Lambda, S3, DynamoDB, Textract, and API Gateway. Furthermore, I improved my teamwork, communication, problem-solving, and time management skills while collaborating on real projects.

### 5. Company Culture and Team Spirit

One of the most impressive aspects of the program was the strong sense of teamwork. Team members were always willing to support one another and work together to solve challenges. The positive and encouraging atmosphere motivated me to learn continuously and contribute effectively to the project.

### 6. Internship Support and Benefits

The internship program was well organized, with a structured learning path, useful learning materials, and regular knowledge-sharing sessions. These resources helped interns gain practical experience and better prepare for future careers in the technology industry.

## Additional Questions

### What was your favorite part of the internship?

My favorite part was the opportunity to work directly with AWS services in a real-world project environment. It allowed me to understand how cloud technologies are applied in practice and helped bridge the gap between academic knowledge and industry requirements.

### What do you think should be improved for future interns?

I believe the program could include more advanced technical workshops and career-sharing sessions from industry professionals. These activities would provide additional insights into career development and emerging technologies.

### Would you recommend this internship program to your friends? Why?

Yes, I would highly recommend this program. It offers valuable hands-on experience with cloud technologies, practical project work, and strong mentorship support. The program is an excellent opportunity for students who want to gain industry-relevant skills and experience.

## Suggestions and Expectations

### Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?

I would suggest organizing more collaborative mini-projects and technical challenges throughout the program. These activities could encourage interns to apply their knowledge more frequently and improve teamwork skills.

### Would you like to continue participating in this program in the future?

Yes. Given the opportunity, I would be interested in joining more advanced training programs or projects related to AWS Cloud, Artificial Intelligence, and Serverless Computing to further enhance my technical expertise.

### Additional Comments

I would like to express my sincere gratitude to the mentors, team administrators, and everyone involved in the FCAJ program. The internship has been an extremely valuable learning experience and has significantly contributed to my personal and professional growth. It has also strengthened my motivation to pursue a career in Cloud Computing and modern software development.
